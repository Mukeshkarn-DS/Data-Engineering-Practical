# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and constructive experience for everyone.

Participants are expected to contribute in good faith and to focus criticism on ideas, code, documentation, and technical decisions rather than individuals.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of expected behavior include:

- Being respectful and professional.
- Providing constructive feedback.
- Accepting differing technical opinions.
- Focusing on solutions and learning.
- Respecting project boundaries and educational context.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, threats, sexualized behavior, publishing private information, and other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, commits, or pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct.

Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent restriction from project participation.

## Reporting

Report serious conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact information listed in the repository profile. Do not include sensitive personal information in public issues.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant principles.
